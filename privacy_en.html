Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This app (the "App") shows weather and your calendar together. The developer (the "Developer") respects your privacy and follows applicable data-protection laws. This policy explains what information the App uses, where it is stored and who receives it.

1. At a glance

  • There is no sign-up or login. The App does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.
  • The Developer runs no servers for the App and does not collect or store your information.
  • Location and calendar information is used only to provide the App's features, and your calendar events never leave your device through the App.

2. Information the App uses, and why

  • Location (precise and approximate): used to show weather, air quality, UV and sunrise/sunset information for where you are. You can turn location permission off at any time in your device settings; the App then shows a default region.
  • Calendar information (event titles, times, locations, notes): used to display the events on your device in the App, and to write changes to your device calendar when you add, edit or delete an event in the App.
  • Google account and calendar sync: the App does not sign in to your Google account and does not use Google APIs. If a Google account you added in your device settings syncs its calendar to your device, the App reads and writes that device calendar. Syncing with Google's servers is handled by your device (Android) and Google Calendar; the App never receives your Google password or credentials. To tell calendars apart on screen, the App reads the account name shown in your device's calendar list (such as a Google email address) and displays it on your device only.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you attach a photo to an event note. The App handles only photos you take or choose yourself.
  • Notifications: used to show notifications on your device when you turn them on.
  • Search text: the place name you type when you search for a saved location.

3. Where information is stored, and for how long

  • Your settings (language, units, which weather items to show), saved locations, recent weather and air-quality cache, and event-note photos are stored only on your device.
  • They are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. You can also delete saved locations and note photos inside the App.
  • Calendar events themselves are stored in your device calendar, and events synced with a Google account are also stored on Google's servers. That is handled by your device and Google Calendar, not by the App, and Google's privacy policy applies. When you add, edit or delete an event in the App, the change may sync to Google through your device calendar.

4. Information sent to outside services

To fetch weather and related data, the App sends requests to the services below. Requests contain only what is needed, and the Developer does not receive or store their contents.

  • Korea Meteorological Administration API Hub: receives your location (as grid coordinates) to look up the weather.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): receives coordinates or your search text for weather outside Korea and for place search.
  • Korea Environment Corporation (AirKorea): receives the name of your area (city/county level) to look up air quality.
  • Google Calendar (device sync): not sent by the App itself; events written to your device calendar may be synced with your Google account.
  • Your device's location-name service: may be handled by your device provider (for example Google) to turn coordinates into a place name.

These services may process request data (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

5. Sharing with third parties and processors

Because the Developer does not collect your personal information, the Developer does not share it with third parties or use processors for it.

6. Advertising

The App may show an ad area at the bottom of the screen. The current version includes no third-party advertising service that tracks you. If one is added, this policy will first be updated with what is collected (such as an advertising ID) and how you can opt out.

7. Your rights

  • You can allow or deny location, calendar, camera and notification permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • You can delete everything the App stores by uninstalling it or clearing its data.
  • For any privacy question or request, contact us at the address below.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 14 (or under 13 in some regions) and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Security

The App uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) when it contacts outside services, and it does not store your information outside your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text and effective date will be shown in the App. Changes that are unfavorable to you will be announced before they take effect.
